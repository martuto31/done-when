What a person checks before a release, newest first: one heading per shipped task that changes what a user sees or
does, with 2–3 steps each ("open X, do Y, expect Z"). The task's own commit adds its entry. After the release, the
checked entries move to `TEST-BY-HAND-archive.md`.
